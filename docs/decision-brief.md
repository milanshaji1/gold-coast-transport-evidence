# Where should further road-safety investigation begin?

Start with the 20-cell count shortlist. Clustering did not improve capture at this budget, and the data cannot identify the highest-risk roads per trip.

The 2020–2024 source contains 8,142 casualty crashes, including 3,407 serious crash events and 4,079 serious casualties. Serious crashes are events classified as Fatal or Hospitalisation; serious casualties are people killed or hospitalised. A fixed 500 m grid covers the current Gold Coast boundary. Rankings use serious crashes during 2021–2023; the held-out comparison uses 2024.

| 2024 evaluation | Top 10 cells | Top 20 cells | Top 40 cells |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Count baseline | 30 / 695 (4.32%) | 55 / 695 (7.91%) | 82 / 695 (11.80%) |
| Density-filtered counts | 30 / 695 (4.32%) | 54 / 695 (7.77%) | 83 / 695 (11.94%) |

At 20 cells, clustering captured one fewer serious crash. The small differences across all three budgets do not establish that either method is better. The 20 cells cover 5 km² nominally and 4.82 km² within the boundary. Thirteen of the 20 count-selected cells overlap the previous three-year fit's shortlist. Most serious crashes in 2024 occurred outside the shortlist.

The Gamma-Poisson experiment was rejected for decisions about individual sites. In the training period, 84.85% of grid cells had no serious crashes. Pooling road and non-road land can give high overall interval coverage without reliable estimates for individual sites. Using the same prior for every cell also leaves the ranking unchanged.

Two serious crashes fall outside the present boundary but remain in source-LGA totals and evaluation denominators. Both fall in boundary-intersecting grid cells. No missing-coordinate exclusions occur in this snapshot.

Next investigation requires traffic exposure, road and intersection context, engineering inspection, existing treatments and consultation. Counts do not measure per-trip risk, establish causes or determine a treatment. This independent portfolio study is not commissioned or endorsed by the City.
